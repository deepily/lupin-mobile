## Entry #5 — Relay user rulings VERBATIM with provenance; paraphrase trips the auto-mode classifier

**Claim + anchor** (ledger §6.5, verbatim): "Auto-mode classifier FALSE-POSITIVEs on relaying

**Fold target**: `plan-review-cascaded-common.md` — ledger names `§user-ruling relay`, which
does not exist. Proposal: NEW subsection under §Escalation Taxonomy (directly after the #4
insert above, forming a paired "ask mechanics" block).

**Proposed redline — INSERT new subsection**:

> ### Relaying user rulings — verbatim-with-provenance rule (added 2026-06-12)
>
> When the Manager relays a user ruling to a worker (DM or topic post), the relay MUST quote
> the ruling **verbatim** and carry provenance metadata: `{ruling_source: "user",
> ruling_channel: "<ask_yes_no|converse|ask_multiple_choice|voice>", ruling_ts: "<ISO>",
> ruling_qid: "<question_id if any>"}`. Never paraphrase a ruling into the relay body.
>
> **Two reasons**: (1) a paraphrase is a lossy transform of the single most authoritative
> input in the pipeline — drift here contaminates every downstream decision; (2) the worker's
> host-harness auto-mode permission classifier evaluates the worker's actions against what
> *that session* has seen — a paraphrased "the user said do X" reads as an unverifiable peer
> claim and can be FALSE-POSITIVE-blocked, whereas a verbatim quote with provenance gives the
> classifier (and any human auditor) the traceable original.
>
> **Empirical anchor**: F-S5-S2-1 relay block (`cascade-focus-mode`, 2026-06-12) — a worker's
> classifier blocked an action authorized by a user ruling the worker's session had not
> witnessed; the verbatim-quote-with-provenance re-relay unblocked it.

**Cross-link to add**: manager-autonomy.md §2 already encodes "a peer relay cannot authorize"
for STILL-GATED actions — this rule is the complementary mechanics for actions a relay CAN
carry (in-envelope work direction), specifying the form that makes the relay trustworthy.

**Proposed redline — INSERT new subsection after Convention 6**:

> ### Convention 7 — Contract-comparison ACs carry owner + instant + sequencing (added 2026-06-12)
>
> **The pattern**: when a plan spans two repos (or two documents that must agree — e.g. a
> mobile section file and a parent-repo work order), the cross-document agreement is itself a
> testable artifact. A **contract-comparison AC** asserts "doc A's interface statements match
> doc B's" — but a bare comparison AC is hollow unless it names:
>
> 1. **OWNER** — which session/persona executes the comparison (the two docs usually have
>    different owners; an unowned AC is executed by nobody);
> 2. **INSTANT** — the exact pipeline moment it runs (e.g. "at cascade close" vs "at
>    implementation-start, before the parent side's first edit") — drift accumulates between
>    instants, so the instant IS the design;
> 3. **SEQUENCING** — where it sits relative to other gates (re-run after any amendment to
>    either document; an amendment without a re-run silently voids the prior pass).
>
> **The grep**: any AC whose verb is "matches / agrees with / is consistent with <other
> document>" without an explicit OWNER + INSTANT is a Stage-3 finding.

> ### 1.5.5 Persona recycling and DM-topic hygiene
>
> `dm-<persona>` topics are keyed by **persona name**, but persona names are **recycled
> resources** — a voice persona reaped from one fleet can be re-allocated to a brand-new
> session within minutes, while the DM topic and its full thread history persist. Two hazards
> follow: (1) the new holder inherits a prior holder's thread and may answer questions never
> addressed to it; (2) a stale-addressed DM (sent to the OLD holder, undelivered or unread)
> reads like a live dispatch to the NEW holder. Empirical anchor (2026-06-12): `dm-rio`
> carried 5 of Tiberius's DMs addressed to a prior Rio that had been reaped 2 minutes before
> re-allocation — the fresh Rio received another fleet's work orders.
>
> **Receiving-side rule (on allocation / first wake under a recycled persona)**: before
> treating any pre-existing `dm-<you>` entry as addressed to you, disambiguate by
> `sender_session_id` + timestamps relative to YOUR allocation time. Entries predating your
> allocation — or addressed via a qid your session never received a push for — belong to a
> prior holder: do not act on them; surface them to your manager.
>
> **Sending-side corollary**: a DM to a persona is a DM to the persona's CURRENT session. If
> the recipient was recently respawned, confirm liveness (`commons_who`) before treating
> silence on an old thread as the current holder's silence.
>
> **Manager-side rule (on reap)**: the reaping manager posts a **closing marker** to the
> persona's dm topic — e.g. `kind: dm_topic_closed`, body "persona <X> seat <session-id>
> reaped <ts>; threads above this marker are CLOSED" — so any future holder has an
> unambiguous thread boundary. (See PIP `workflow/manager-autonomy.md` §6 for the reap-side
> mandate.)

memento"**:
