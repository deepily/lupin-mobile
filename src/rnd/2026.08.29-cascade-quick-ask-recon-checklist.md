  (no Android SDK or `adb` on the dev box). Runbook: `src/rnd/2026.06.12-poc-laptop-build-runbook.md`.
- **Style**: Dart camelCase for variables and functions, PascalCase for classes; Rick wants spaces
  inside parentheses and square brackets; double quotes for strings.
- **Flask is banned.** Not a stylistic note.
- **Doc-viewer links**: scope segment is `lupin-mobile`, path-prefixed, `?scope=` is retired and
