## What a failure is worth

**Paste the item number and what you saw** — that is a bug for me, not a discussion.

Items **10 through 13** are the four no test can reach, so a surprise there is the most valuable thing this
walk-through can produce. Items 1 through 9 are mostly confirmation; a failure in one of those means something
is wrong where 409 tests said it was right, which is worth knowing for a different reason.

⚠️ **And if nothing fails, that is a real result, not a formality.** It moves all five from *"green in a
simulator"* to *"seen working"* — the gap §2 of the check-in briefing exists to name.
